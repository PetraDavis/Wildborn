How to play

  • You are a Vesperfox. Eat, drink and stay safe to grow from a tiny Hatchling into a huge Elder with three glowing tails.
  • Hunt: Puffhops hop in the grass, Brookfins swim in the river, Glimmermoths flutter by the flowers and Pebblebeetles crawl near rocks. Big Moonharts are only for Adults. Aim yourself and click to pounce straight ahead. Most prey takes a few bites to bring down, and a pounce bites harder than a normal bite. Hurt prey runs, and some fight back: Puffhops kick, Pebblebeetles pinch, and a Moonhart kicks hard. Your bite gets stronger as you grow. Sneaking with C lets you get closer.
  • Eat: a catch leaves a carcass. Hold E to eat it bit by bit, or press F to pick it up and carry it. Food goes bad after a few minutes, wherever you leave it.
  • Danger: Duskhawks circle overhead and dive at small foxes in the open: hide under trees or go into a burrow. At night Shadeprowlers hunt. Bite back and they may run, or beat them for a big meal.
  • Drink: walk to the river edge and hold E.
  • Burrows: look for big grassy hills with a dark hole at the bottom (there's one right by where you hatch, and the holes glow softly at night). Walk in and follow the tunnel down to a safe room. Some hills have side tunnels to more rooms. Nothing can follow you in there.
  • Sleep: press Z in a burrow's room or under a tree. You heal, time passes, and if you faint you wake up in the last burrow you slept in.
  • Move: WASD or arrows, Shift run, Space jump, X sit. Click the game to look with the mouse; Esc frees it. Scroll to zoom.
  • Preview test key: G gives you a burst of growth so you can see every stage quickly.
