Murkwisp

A living shadow that burrows by melting into the ground. Drag to spin, scroll to zoom.

How it works
  • No legs: it drifts low over the grass
  • Melts into the earth like spilled ink, leaving a dark puddle
  • Slides along underground as a dark ripple, eyes peeking up now and then
  • Gets more wisps, brighter eyes and (as an Elder) a crown of smoke as it grows
