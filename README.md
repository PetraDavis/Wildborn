# Wildborn

A 3D browser game where **you are the creature**. Hatch as a baby, hunt, drink, sleep and grow up into an elder in your own wild world. A cousin of [Wyrmwild](https://github.com/PetraDavis/Wyrmwild).

## Play

Open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge or Firefox (or the GitHub Pages site once it is turned on).

- **WASD** move, **Shift** run, **C** sneak, **Space** jump
- **Click** pounce / bite, **E** eat or drink, **F** carry, **Z** sleep, **X** sit, **H** help

## What's in it so far

- **Moonlit Meadow**: hills, a river, trees, five burrows to sleep in, and a day-night cycle
- **Vesperfox** (playable): grows from Hatchling to Youngling, Adult and Elder
- Prey (Puffhop, Brookfin, Glimmermoth, Pebblebeetle, Moonhart) and predators (Duskhawk, Shadeprowler); every creature has its own health and damage

## Creature previews (not playable yet)

- `previews/emberkit.html`: Emberkit, a long-tailed ember spirit that flies like a wingless serpent dragon and climbs
- `previews/murkwisp.html`: Murkwisp, a living shadow that burrows by melting into the ground
